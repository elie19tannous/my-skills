# Divorce Preparation Checklist

## Before you file — get advice on forum
The filing race (merotz salchuyot) can lock in which court hears property/support/custody. Decide *with a lawyer* where to file each ancillary matter first. Do not file blind.

## Documents to gather
- Marriage certificate; IDs of both spouses and children.
- Asset picture: bank/savings, pensions and study funds (keren hishtalmon), property (Tabu extracts), vehicles, business interests, debts.
- Which assets predate the marriage / were gifts or inheritances (and evidence).
- Income proof for both spouses (pay slips, tax returns) — needed for support.
- Parenting facts: current arrangement, schools, special needs.
- Any prenup / relationship agreement (heskem mamon) and its approval record.

## Interim measures to consider early
- Temporary child/spousal support order.
- Interim parenting-time order.
- Asset-freezing / lien to prevent dissipation.
- Exit restriction where flight risk exists.

## Process reminders
- Family disputes route through a mandatory information/assessment meeting (MAHUT) and the assistance unit (yehidat siyua) before full litigation.
- A negotiated divorce agreement (heskem gerushin) must be given force of judgment and the gett completed to be binding.

## Gett
- The Rabbinical Court arranges the gett; the marriage ends only on completion.
- If a spouse refuses (sarvanut gett), ask the court to invoke sanctions under the 1995 enforcement law. Do not buy the gett with property concessions without counsel.

## Questions to bring to the lawyer
- Where should each ancillary matter be filed, and how fast?
- Is any pre-marital / inherited asset exposed via commingling or intent to share?
- What is a realistic child-support figure given our incomes and parenting split (post-2017 case law)?
- Is a consent divorce realistic here?
