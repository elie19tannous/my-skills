# Cancellation Decision Guide

Use this to pick the correct cancellation regime, window, fee, and shipping rule.

## Step 1 — How was the deal made?

| Situation | Regime |
|-----------|--------|
| Phone, internet, catalog, mail (remote) | Distance transaction, section 14ג |
| Door-to-door / off business premises (rochelet) | Off-premises transaction, section 14ג1 |
| In a physical store, in person | General cancellation regulations (bitul iska), 2010 |

## Step 2 — Window

| Regime | Window |
|--------|--------|
| Distance/off-premises (goods) | Order date → 14 days after receiving goods |
| Distance/off-premises (services) | Order date → 14 days after contract or written disclosure, whichever later |
| Distance/off-premises, protected consumer* | 4 months (if transaction included a conversation) |
| General 2010 regulations (goods) | 14 days from purchase or receipt, whichever later |
| General 2010 regulations (services) | 14 days, if performance has not begun |

\* protected consumer = person with disability, senior (65+), or new immigrant.

## Step 3 — Is it a change of mind or a defect?

| Reason | Cancellation fee | Return shipping |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Change of mind | Lower of 5% of price or 100 NIS | Consumer may bear it |
| Defective / not as described | None | Seller bears it |

## Step 4 — Is the item excluded?

Excluded from the general 14-day return: perishables, custom/made-to-order goods, opened copyable media/software, opened intimate/hygiene items, medicines/supplements, certain travel/lodging/tickets (which follow the distance-sales cancellation rules instead). Verify the current list — it is amended periodically.

## Step 5 — Refund

Seller refunds within 14 days of the cancellation notice, using the original payment method, minus at most the capped fee.
