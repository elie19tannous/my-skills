# Israeli Limitation Periods (Hitiyashnut)

Reference for the Limitation Law, 5718-1958 (Chok HaHitiyashnut). Verify current
text against the primary source — this is a planning aid, not a substitute for a
lawyer's assessment.

## Base periods

| Matter | Hebrew | Period | Basis |
|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| General civil claim (not land) | תביעה שאינה במקרקעין | 7 years | Limitation Law s.5 |
| Unregistered land (mekarke'in lo mesudarim) | מקרקעין לא מוסדרים | 15 years | Limitation Law s.5 |
| Registered land (mekarke'in mesudarim) | מקרקעין מוסדרים | 25 years | Limitation Law s.5 |

A specific statute may set its OWN limitation period (often shorter) for a
particular cause of action. Always check whether the governing law overrides the
general 7 years.

## When the clock starts (accrual — s.6)

Default: the day the cause of action arose (nolad ilat hatvia). Modifiers:

| Rule | Hebrew | Effect |
|------|--------|--------|
| Discovery rule (s.8) | כלל הגילוי | For facts the plaintiff neither knew nor should have known, the clock starts on discovery — subject to an outer cap for some claims |
| Minority (s.10) | קטינות | Does not run against a minor; typically starts at majority (18) |
| Legal incapacity (s.11) | פסול דין | Suspended while the plaintiff lacks legal capacity |
| Fraud / deceit (s.7) | תרמית או הונאה | Starts when the plaintiff discovered the fraud |
| Acknowledgment of the right (s.9) | הודאה בקיום זכות | A written acknowledgment by the defendant restarts the clock from the acknowledgment |

## Suspension (tolling)

Events that PAUSE an already-running clock (do not restart it):
- Plaintiff's minority or legal incapacity.
- Certain periods where the defendant was abroad or the plaintiff was prevented
  from suing by force majeure (check the statute's exact conditions).

## Practice notes

- Do NOT assume a flat 7-year calendar count. Discovery, minority, incapacity,
  fraud, and acknowledgment routinely move the start or restart the clock.
- Land claims are 15 or 25 years, not 7. Confirm registration status.
- A limitation defense must generally be RAISED by the defendant at the earliest
  opportunity; it is not applied by the court on its own motion in the same way
  everywhere — but from the plaintiff's side, treat the period as hard.
- File with a safe margin. Never file at the very edge of a limitation period.
